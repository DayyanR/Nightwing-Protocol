📸
Take Weekly Photos
Same lighting, same pose, every Sunday morning. You're vain — use it. Visual proof of progress keeps motivation locked in. You've been training since February; the changes are there, you're just not capturing them.
🥩
Protein First, Every Meal
0.8–1g per lb of bodyweight daily. This is the most important nutritional variable. Eat protein first at every meal — it auto-regulates calories and preserves every lb of muscle you're building.
⏰
Shorter Rest = Faster Lean
Strict 60s rest in Phase 1. This keeps heart rate elevated, burns more calories per session, and creates more metabolic stress — which is what makes muscles look "hard." Use a timer, not a vibe.
🌙
Sleep is the Cheat Code
7–9 hrs. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep — this is literally when your muscles grow and fat burns. Less than 6 hrs doubles cortisol, which stores fat around the midsection specifically.
💧
3–4L Water Daily
Dehydrated muscles look flat and soft. Hydrated muscles look full, hard, and defined. This is the cheapest and fastest way to look better — and most people are chronically under-hydrated.
📈
Log Every Session
You're already doing this in your app — keep it up. Progressive overload (adding 1 rep or small weight weekly) is the mechanism behind muscle growth. Without logs, you're guessing. With logs, you're engineering.
