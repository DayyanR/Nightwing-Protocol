The Golden Rules
🥩
Protein First, Every Single Meal
Before you eat anything else, get your protein in. 135g per day minimum. This preserves every lb of muscle while you cut fat. It also fills you up — you'll naturally eat less of everything else.
⏰
Biggest Carbs Go Post-Workout
You train fasted so your post-workout breakfast is the most important meal. Load carbs immediately after training. On rest days (Sunday), keep carbs under 130g — no training means no need for a full tank.
🌙
Stop Eating by 10PM
Except for your casein/cottage cheese. You're waking at 5am to train — that means lights out by 9:30–10pm. Sleep is when you recover from fasted training. Late-night eating and poor sleep are the two things that will stall your results fastest.
🍬
Treats Are Allowed — With Rules
Dark chocolate, gummy bears, a cookie — fit it in your calories and you're fine. Deprivation causes binges. One treat per day keeps you consistent for 133 days. Consistency beats perfection every time.
💧
3–4L Water Every Day
Muscles look flat when dehydrated and full when hydrated. At your size, 3L is the floor. Drink a full glass first thing in the morning before coffee — it rehydrates you after 8 hrs without water.
